# Receipt Analyser — Product Demo Video Script

Full shot-by-shot script for a screen-recorded product demo, built for Clipchamp (free,
built into Windows 11). Every scene is grounded in the REAL, live product — nothing staged
or mocked up. Total runtime target: **55–65 seconds** (ideal for landing page / social).

---

## Video specs

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 for website/YouTube, ALSO export a 9:16 crop for Instagram/TikTok/Reels |
| Resolution | 1920×1080 minimum |
| Style | Real screen capture (not animated/cartoon) — the product's honesty IS the pitch |
| Music | Light, upbeat, no lyrics (Clipchamp's free built-in music library has several "corporate/tech" tracks that fit — search "modern" or "tech" in its music panel) |
| Voiceover | Optional — the script works with on-screen captions alone if you don't want to record narration. Lines are written short enough to work either way. |
| Cursor | Keep your mouse cursor visible and move deliberately — fast/jittery cursor movement reads as unpolished |

---

## Pre-recording checklist

1. Clean browser: close other tabs, hide bookmarks bar, use a fresh/incognito-style window so no personal data leaks into frame.
2. Use a **guest account or fresh test signup** for recording (not your real Barat account) — same reasoning as our earlier live testing: never show anyone else's data on camera, and start with a genuinely empty dashboard so numbers build up naturally.
3. Have 2–3 REAL receipts ready to photograph/send (a coffee receipt, a grocery receipt, a fuel receipt) — variety sells "it works on anything."
4. Set your phone's Telegram chat with the bot open and ready, screen-recorded via your phone's own screen recorder (or mirror your phone to the PC if you want everything in one recording).
5. Zoom your browser to 110–125% before recording — small dashboard text reads poorly in a compressed video.

## Scene-by-scene script

### Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:05)
**Show:** Landing page hero (`localhost:3001` or your live domain once it exists) — the headline "Turn any receipt into structured data, instantly."
**Caption:** `Still typing receipts into a spreadsheet?`
**Voiceover (optional):** "What if your receipts just... organized themselves?"
**Action:** Slow zoom into the Telegram-window hero graphic on the right side of Landing.

### Scene 2 — The real Telegram scan (0:05–0:18)
**Show:** Real phone screen (or mirrored), inside the actual `@Receiptreader56bot` chat.
**Action, in order:**
1. Open Telegram, tap into the bot chat.
2. Tap the photo/camera icon, take (or select) a real receipt photo.
3. Send it.
4. Show the bot's real "🧾 Reading your receipt… Please hold on a moment 🙏" reply appearing.
5. Show the real "✅ Expense Captured" reply landing seconds later, with merchant/date/total/category filled in.
**Caption:** `Snap a photo. Send it. Done.`
**Voiceover:** "Just send a photo on Telegram — no app to install, no forms to fill."

### Scene 3 — The extraction, called out (0:18–0:26)
**Show:** Zoom into the bot's "✅ Expense Captured" reply text.
**Action:** Use Clipchamp's zoom/highlight tool to circle or zoom into each extracted field one at a time: Merchant → Date → Total → Category.
**Caption (appears one at a time, matching the zoom):** `Merchant ✓`  →  `Amount ✓`  →  `Category ✓`
**Voiceover:** "It reads the merchant, the total, even sorts it into a category — automatically."

### Scene 4 — Cut to the dashboard, receipt already there (0:26–0:34)
**Show:** Switch to the browser, Overview page. Click **Refresh** (or reload) so the new receipt visibly appears/updates in front of the viewer.
**Action:** Show the "Receipts processed" counter and "% needed no edit" numbers ticking up, and the category donut/monthly chart updating.
**Caption:** `It's already on your dashboard.`
**Voiceover:** "By the time you put your phone down, it's already on your dashboard."

### Scene 5 — Overview page tour (0:34–0:44)
**Show:** Overview page, calmly scrolling/panning through:
1. Automation Impact panel (receipts processed, % needed no edit, hours saved)
2. Total spend + total bills numbers
3. "Expenses by Month" bar chart
4. "Spend by category" donut
**Caption:** `Real totals. Real trends. Zero spreadsheets.`
**Voiceover:** "See exactly where your money's going — by month, by category, at a glance."

### Scene 6 — Activity: the full bill list (0:44–0:52)
**Show:** Click into Activity. Show the table: voucher number, merchant, date, amount, category, status columns, with at least 2–3 real rows visible.
**Action:** Hover/click one row to show it expand or open a detail view if that's built; otherwise just a clean scroll through the list.
**Caption:** `Every receipt, searchable and organized.`
**Voiceover:** "Every single receipt — searchable, filterable, always there when you need it."

### Scene 7 — Close / CTA (0:52–1:00)
**Show:** Back to Landing page, hero + "Try for Free" button, cursor moving toward it (don't need to actually click through).
**Caption:** `Try it free — no card required.`
**Voiceover:** "Try it yourself — takes less than a minute to get started."
**End card:** Logo/wordmark + domain (once you have one) held for 2 seconds.

## Caption style notes (for Clipchamp)

- Use Clipchamp's built-in caption/text tool, not a hand-drawn overlay — keep font consistent across all scenes (pick one bold sans-serif, e.g. Montserrat or Inter, and reuse it every time).
- Keep captions **on screen for at least 2 seconds** even in fast cuts — social platforms are often watched muted, so captions ARE the narration for a large share of viewers.
- Match caption color to your brand's blue (`#2563eb`, the same blue from the Landing page) for a consistent identity between the video and the site.

## Music suggestion

Clipchamp's free library → search "Tech" or "Corporate Upbeat" → pick something under 90 seconds so you don't need to trim/loop it. Keep volume low (~15–20%) under any voiceover, or ~40% if captions-only with no voiceover.

## Export settings

Export at 1080p, then use Clipchamp's built-in crop tool to also produce a 9:16 vertical version for Instagram Reels/TikTok/YouTube Shorts — same footage, just reframed on the phone-screen and dashboard close-ups (skip wide landing-page shots in the vertical cut, they won't read well on a phone).
